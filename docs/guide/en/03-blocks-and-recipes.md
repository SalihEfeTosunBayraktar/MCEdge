# Blocks, recipes and creative tabs

## A block

Create a **Block** element. Give it a texture (one for all sides, or one per side), a sound, a hardness and the tool needed to mine it. The model preview shows how it looks.

## A recipe

1. Create a **Recipe** element and choose its type: crafting, smelting, blasting, smoking, campfire, stonecutting, smithing or brewing.
2. For crafting, click a slot of the grid to choose an item or a tag; **None** empties a slot. Tick **Shapeless** when the order does not matter.
3. Choose the result and how many it gives.

A tag such as `#minecraft:planks` accepts any item of the tag. Your own tags are made in **Element tags**.

## A creative tab

Create a **Creative tab**, choose its icon item and order the items it shows. Items and blocks choose their tab in their own editor.

## Translations

**Localization** lists every text of the mod per language. Add a language, then fill in the names; the filter shows the missing ones.
